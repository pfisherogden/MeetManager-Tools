# Coach & Team Manager Guide

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the MMTools platform! As a Coach or Team Manager, you need quick, reliable access to meet information, team rosters, swimmer events, and results. MMTools is designed to provide you with all this data in a clean, accessible format, accessible from any device.

This guide will walk you through how to use the MMTools web dashboard to effectively manage your team during a swim meet.

---

## 2. Accessing Your Team's Data

MMTools is a cloud-based web application. You do not need to install any software on your computer to view the meet data.

### 2.1. Navigating to the Dashboard
1. The Meet Director will provide you with a URL to access the meet (e.g., `https://mmtools-frontend-[hash]-uw.a.run.app`).
2. Depending on the meet's privacy settings, you may need to log in using your Google account.
3. Once logged in, you will land on the **Dashboard**, which provides a high-level overview of the entire meet.

*(Screenshot placeholder: The main Dashboard showing the total number of athletes, events, and teams.)*
`[Image: Main Dashboard Overview]`

### 2.2. Finding Your Team
1. On the left navigation sidebar, click on **Teams**.
2. This page lists every club participating in the current meet.
3. You can use the search bar at the top to quickly find your team by name or club code (e.g., "SHRK" or "Shark Aquatics").
4. Click on your team's row to view the detailed **Team Profile**.

## 3. Reviewing Swimmer Entries

The Team Profile page is your central hub for managing your athletes during the meet.

### 3.1. The Team Profile Page
- The top of the page displays your team's total athlete count and overall score (if the meet is scored).
- Below the summary, you will find a complete roster of all swimmers on your team who are entered in the meet.

*(Screenshot placeholder: The Team Profile page showing the roster and total points.)*
`[Image: Team Profile Page]`

### 3.2. Checking Individual Entries
1. In your team roster, click on the name of a specific swimmer.
2. This opens the **Athlete Profile**.
3. Here, you can see every event the swimmer is entered in, including:
    - **Event Number & Name** (e.g., "Event 15: Girls 7-8 25 Yard Freestyle")
    - **Heat & Lane Assignments** (Crucial for getting them to the blocks on time!)
    - **Seed Time** (Their entry time)
    - **Final Time & Place** (Updated once results are processed by the computer table).

*(Screenshot placeholder: An Athlete Profile showing their schedule, heat/lane assignments, and seed times.)*

## 4. Understanding Relays

Relays can be complex to manage on the pool deck. MMTools makes it easy to see exactly who is swimming in which order.

### 4.1. Finding Relay Entries
1. On the left navigation sidebar, click on **Relays**.
2. This page lists all relay events in the meet.
3. You can filter this list by your specific team using the dropdown or search bar.

### 4.2. Viewing Relay Legs
- For each relay entry, the app clearly displays the four swimmers assigned to the relay in their running order (Leg 1, Leg 2, Leg 3, Leg 4).
- This ensures you know exactly which athlete needs to be ready at which block.

## 5. Live Results & Scoring (If Applicable)

If the Meet Director is actively syncing results from the timing system back into MMTools during the meet, you can track performance in real-time.

### 5.1. Checking Event Results
1. Click on **Events** in the left sidebar.
2. Select an event that has already concluded.
3. The Event View will display the swimmers ordered by their final placement, along with their official times and any points scored.
4. *Note: If a swimmer was Disqualified, a "DQ" badge and the specific infraction code will be displayed next to their name.*

### 5.2. Checking Team Scores
1. If the meet is being scored, click on **Scores** in the sidebar.
2. This page provides a live leaderboard of all teams, broken down by individual points and relay points.

## 6. Accessing PDF Reports

While the interactive web dashboard is powerful, sometimes you just need a piece of paper on the wet pool deck.

### 6.1. Downloading the Coach's Program
1. The Meet Director will generate a "Meet Program" PDF prior to the meet.
2. This document is typically a dense, 2-column format designed specifically to save paper while giving coaches a complete overview of every heat.
3. If the Meet Director has shared access, you can navigate to the **Reports** section and download the **Coaches Meet Program** directly to your device for printing.
