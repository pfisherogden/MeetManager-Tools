# Stroke & Turn Judge Guide

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the MMTools Mobile Judge App! As a Stroke & Turn (S&T) Judge, your primary responsibility is to observe swimmers and ensure they comply with the technical rules of their strokes. When an infraction occurs, you must record a Disqualification (DQ) code and communicate it to the meet referee and the computer table.

This application is designed specifically for use on the pool deck. It works **offline**, meaning you don't need a constant Wi-Fi or cellular connection while judging. You only need a connection at the beginning of the session to download the meet program and at the end of your shift (or when convenient) to sync your recorded DQs back to the main computer.

---

## 2. Getting Started: Installation & Setup

Because pool deck Wi-Fi is notoriously unreliable, this app is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA). This means you install it directly from your web browser without going to the App Store or Google Play.

### 2.1. Loading the App
1. At the beginning of your shift, find the Meet Director or the Computer Team desk.
2. They will display a large **QR Code** on their screen.
3. Open your smartphone's camera and point it at the QR code.
4. Tap the link that appears on your screen. This will open the Mobile Judge App in your web browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).
5. *Note: Scanning the QR code automatically loads the current meet's events, heats, and swimmer rosters onto your phone.*

### 2.2. Installing to Home Screen (Crucial Step)
To ensure the app works offline and doesn't get accidentally closed:

**On iPhone (Safari):**
1. Tap the "Share" icon at the bottom of the screen (a square with an arrow pointing up).
2. Scroll down the menu and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
3. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.
4. Close Safari and open the new "Judge App" icon on your home screen.

**On Android (Chrome):**
1. Tap the three-dot menu icon in the top right corner.
2. Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.
3. Follow the on-screen prompts.
4. Open the app from your home screen.

## 3. Navigating the Meet Program

Once loaded, the app defaults to the **Program View**, which acts as your digital clipboard.

### 3.1. The Program View
- The main screen lists the events in order (e.g., "Event 1: Girls 8 & Under 100 Medley Relay").
- Under each event, you will see the scheduled Heats.
- Tap the **"Next Event"** or **"Previous Event"** arrows at the top of the screen to quickly jump between events if the meet is moving fast.

*(Screenshot placeholder: The Program View displaying a list of events and heats.)*

### 3.2. Viewing Swimmers in a Heat
1. Tap on a specific Heat (e.g., "Heat 1" under Event 1).
2. You will be taken to the **Event View**. This screen lists the lanes and the swimmers (or relay teams) assigned to those lanes.
3. Empty lanes are clearly marked with `(Empty)`.

## 4. Recording a Disqualification (DQ)

When you observe an infraction, you must record it accurately against the correct swimmer.

### 4.1. Initiating a DQ
1. In the Event View, locate the lane of the swimmer who committed the infraction.
2. Tap the large **"TAP TO DQ"** button next to their name.
3. This opens the **DQ Code Selection Sheet**.

*(Screenshot placeholder: The DQ Code Selection Sheet popping up over the Event View.)*
`[Image: DQ Code Selection]`

### 4.2. Selecting the Infraction Code
The DQ sheet is organized by stroke category to help you quickly find the correct USA Swimming/NFHS rule code.
1. Select the relevant category tab at the top (e.g., "FLY", "BACK", "BREAST", "FREE", "RELAY", "MISC").
2. Scroll through the list of infractions.
3. Tap the specific rule violation you observed (e.g., "3A: Alternating kick").
4. A checkmark ✅ will appear next to the selected code.
5. (Optional) Add brief notes if the referee requires clarification.

### 4.3. Saving the DQ
1. Once the correct code is selected, tap the **Save** icon (typically a checkmark ✅ at the top of the sheet).
2. The sheet will close. The swimmer's row in the Event View will now be highlighted in **red**, clearly indicating a DQ has been recorded.

## 5. The Offline Queue & Synchronization

Because you are likely working offline, your recorded DQs are saved locally on your phone first.

### 5.1. Checking Your Queue
1. Tap the **Queue** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. This screen shows a list of all DQs you have recorded during your shift that have *not yet* been sent to the computer table.
3. Review this list periodically to ensure accuracy. If you made a mistake, you can tap the trash can icon next to an entry to delete it.

*(Screenshot placeholder: The Offline Queue showing a list of pending DQs with delete options.)*
`[Image: Offline Queue List]`

### 5.2. Syncing with the Computer Table
When you have a moment, step away from the deck to an area with Wi-Fi or cellular service (e.g., near the admin table).
1. Go to the **Queue** tab.
2. Tap the large **"Sync"** button.
3. The app will attempt to connect to the MMTools server and securely transmit your recorded DQs.
4. Upon success, the queue will clear, and you will see a success message. The computer table now has your data!

---

## 6. Best Practices & Troubleshooting

- **Battery Life**: Ensure your phone is fully charged before your shift. Turn down screen brightness if you are in direct sunlight to save power.
- **Accidental Closure**: If you accidentally swipe the app away, simply reopen it from your home screen. Your downloaded meet program and any un-synced DQs in your offline queue will still be there.
- **"Network Error" on Sync**: If you tap "Sync" and get an error, it means you don't have a strong enough internet connection. Move closer to the admin table's Wi-Fi router or step outside the building to get cellular service, then try again. Do not delete the DQs; they are safe in the queue until the sync succeeds.
