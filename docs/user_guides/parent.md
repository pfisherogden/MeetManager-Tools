# Parent & Spectator Guide

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the MMTools platform! Swim meets can be chaotic and confusing, especially when you are trying to figure out exactly when and where your child is swimming. MMTools is designed to give you clear, real-time access to your swimmer's schedule directly from your smartphone.

This guide will help you navigate the system so you never miss a race.

---

## 2. Finding Your Swimmer

There is no app to download from the App Store; MMTools runs right in your phone's web browser.

### 2.1. Accessing the Meet
1. The host team or Meet Director will provide a link to the meet (e.g., via email, a QR code on the wall, or the team website).
2. Tap the link to open the MMTools web app.
3. *Note: Depending on the meet's privacy rules, you might be asked to "Sign in with Google" before viewing the data.*

### 2.2. Searching for Your Child
1. Once on the main Dashboard, look for the navigation menu (on mobile, this might be behind a "hamburger" icon ☰).
2. Tap on **Athletes**.
3. You will see a search bar at the top of the screen.
4. Type in your child's first or last name. As you type, the list will filter down automatically.
5. Tap on your child's name when it appears.

## 3. Understanding the Athlete Profile

The Athlete Profile page contains everything you need to know about your swimmer's day.

### 3.1. The Schedule
The main section of the profile lists every event your child is entered in. For each entry, you will see:
- **Event Name**: E.g., "Event 15: Girls 9-10 50 Yard Freestyle"
- **Seed Time**: Their previous best time used to place them in a heat.
- **Heat & Lane**: *This is the most important information!* It tells you exactly when they will swim and which lane they will be in.

*(Screenshot placeholder: An Athlete Profile zoomed in on the Heat and Lane assignment numbers.)*
`[Image: Athlete Heat and Lane]`

### 3.2. How to Read "Heat 3, Lane 4"
- **Heat**: Events are divided into heats. Heat 1 swims first, then Heat 2, and so on. Pay attention to the announcer or the scoreboard to know which heat is currently in the water.
- **Lane**: The pool is divided into numbered lanes (usually 1 through 6 or 8). Your child must stand behind their assigned lane block before their heat begins.

---

## 4. Following the Action (Events View)

If you want to see who your child is racing against, or check the results after the race, use the Events view.

### 4.1. Viewing a Specific Event
1. From the navigation menu, tap on **Events**.
2. Scroll through the list to find your child's event number.
3. Tap the event.

### 4.2. Before the Race (Heat Sheets)
- If the race hasn't happened yet, the Event page acts as a "Heat Sheet."
- It will list all the heats in order.
- You can find your child's heat and see the names and entry times of all the other swimmers in that same race.

*(Screenshot placeholder: The Event view showing the swimmers lined up in Heat 3.)*
`[Image: Event Heat Sheet View]`

### 4.3. After the Race (Results)
- If the Meet Director is uploading results during the meet, you can return to this same Event page after the race.
- The view will automatically update to show the final placements, official times, and any points scored.
- *Note: If a swimmer is disqualified, a "DQ" badge will appear next to their name instead of a final time.*

---

## 5. Helpful Tips for Parents

- **Refresh the Page**: If you know results have been announced but you don't see them on your phone, pull down on your screen to refresh the web page.
- **Check Relays**: Sometimes swimmers don't know they are in a relay until the last minute. Tap the **Relays** tab in the menu and search for your team to see if your child's name is listed on a relay squad.
- **Parent Lineups PDF**: In addition to the app, the host team might print "Parent Lineups." These are special PDF reports generated by MMTools that group swimmers by age and gender, making it very easy for bullpen volunteers to round up the 8 & Under kids!
