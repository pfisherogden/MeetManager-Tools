# Swimmer Guide

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the MMTools platform! This web application is your go-to resource on meet day. Instead of crowding around a piece of paper taped to a brick wall to find out when you swim, you can look up your exact schedule, heat, and lane assignments directly on your phone.

This guide will show you how to use MMTools to stay organized and ready for your races.

---

## 2. Finding Your Schedule

MMTools is accessed through a web browser on your phone or computer.

### 2.1. Opening the App
1. Get the meet link from your coach or the meet host.
2. Tap the link to open the MMTools web app in your browser (like Safari or Chrome).
3. If prompted, you may need to log in with your Google account.

### 2.2. Locating Your Profile
1. On the main page, find the navigation menu (usually a ☰ icon in the top corner on phones).
2. Tap on **Athletes**.
3. Use the search bar at the top to type in your name.
4. When your name appears in the list, tap it to open your personal **Athlete Profile**.

## 3. Reading Your Athlete Profile

Your Athlete Profile contains your entire schedule for the day. It's the most important page for you to check.

### 3.1. Your Events List
Under your name and team info, you will see a list of every event you are swimming. Pay close attention to these three things for each event:
1.  **Event Number**: This helps you track the progress of the meet. Listen to the announcer to know what event is currently in the water.
2.  **Heat Number**: This tells you *when* in the event you swim. Wait for the heats before yours to finish before stepping up.
3.  **Lane Number**: This is *where* you swim. You must stand behind the starting block for this specific lane.

*(Screenshot placeholder: An Athlete Profile showing a list of events with Heat and Lane clearly visible.)*
`[Image: Swimmer Event Schedule]`

### 3.2. Seed Times
Next to your heat and lane, you will see your **Seed Time**. This is your entry time. Swimmers in the same heat usually have similar seed times, so expect a good race!

---

## 4. Checking Relays

Sometimes you might be entered in a relay event without realizing it. It's crucial to check!

### 4.1. The Relays Page
1. Open the navigation menu and tap **Relays**.
2. Type your team's name or code (e.g., "SHRK") into the search bar.
3. Look through the list of your team's relays.

### 4.2. Finding Your Leg
- If your team is listed, read the names of the four swimmers under "Leg 1, Leg 2, Leg 3, Leg 4".
- If your name is there, make sure you know which "Leg" (order) you are swimming so you know when to dive in.

## 5. Checking Your Results

After you swim, you'll naturally want to know your official time and how you placed.

### 5.1. Live Updates
If the meet is using live result syncing, you can check your times shortly after your race finishes.
1. Go back to your **Athlete Profile**.
2. Look at the event you just swam. The "Final Time" and "Place" columns will update automatically once the computer team publishes the results.

### 5.2. Checking the Full Event
If you want to see how everyone else in your event did:
1. Tap on the name of the Event from your profile.
2. This opens the **Event View**.
3. You will see a ranked list of all swimmers in that event, their official times, and any points they scored for their team.

## 6. Pro Tips for Meet Day

- **Keep the Tab Open**: Keep the MMTools page open in a tab on your phone browser so you don't have to keep searching for the link.
- **Refresh**: If you know an event just finished but you don't see results, pull down on your screen to refresh the page.
- **Double Check**: Always double-check your Heat and Lane right before you head to the blocks!
