MeetManager Tools

Stroke & Turn Judge App

A mobile-optimized interface designed for deck referees and judges. It allows officials to quickly record disqualifications (DQs), assign them to specific swimmers or relay legs, manage an offline queue for areas with poor connectivity, and sync results back to the main meet database.

Configuration Params: None currently supported.

Meet Program & Results Viewer

A spectator and coach-friendly dashboard providing a real-time view of the meet. Features include heat and lane assignments, seed times, ordered relay swimmer names, live results with final times, and visual medal indicators for top finishers.

Configuration Params: None currently supported.
