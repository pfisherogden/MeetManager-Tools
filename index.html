MMTools

Modern Swimming Meet Orchestration

A suite of lightweight, high-performance web and desktop applications built to streamline swim meet operations, spectator experience, and referee workflow.

Central Controller

MMTools Desktop

The core application for meet managers, coordinators, and deck officials. MMTools simplifies swim meet operations by parsing your meet databases, generating high-fidelity PDF program sheets and CTS scoreboard files, and syncing data in real-time to mobile judge apps on the deck.

Key Benefits:
  • Easy Meet Import: Drag and drop your meet database to instantly load events, entries, and rosters.
  • Fast PDF Reports: Generate beautifully formatted heat sheets, psych sheets, and starter lists in seconds.
  • Real-time Deck Syncing: Push heat/lane updates and sync disqualifications (DQs) instantly with deck officials.
  • Scoreboard Exports: Directly generate CTS and Wahoo! Results scoreboard compatible files.

 macOS Installation Note: Since the application is compiled and packaged securely via GitHub Actions, macOS will show an "unidentified developer" warning on first launch. You can authorize only this specific app without changing your Mac's global security settings using one of these methods:
Method 1: Open Anyway (Standard)
  1. Double-click the downloaded app to trigger the warning dialog, then click Cancel.
  2. Open your Mac's System Settings and navigate to Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the Security section. You will see a note stating: "MM-Tools" was blocked from use because it is not from an identified developer.
  4. Click the Open Anyway button and enter your Mac credentials. This authorizes only MMTools.
Method 2: Remove Quarantine Flag (Terminal)

Alternatively, you can clear the download quarantine flag for MMTools alone. Open your Terminal and run: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MM-Tools.app
